=== Sample Conversation 1 ===
Hiring Manager: Hi, I need to hire a Senior Python Developer for our backend team.
Recruiter: Great! Can you tell me more about the role?
Hiring Manager: We need someone with at least 5 years of experience in Python, Django, and PostgreSQL. They should also know Docker and AWS.
Recruiter: What's the salary range?
Hiring Manager: We're offering between $120,000 and $150,000 depending on experience.
Recruiter: Any specific location preference?
Hiring Manager: Remote is fine, but we prefer US-based candidates.
